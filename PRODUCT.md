# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Teenagers and young indie-game fans in Russia and Eastern Europe (and globally via Itch.io) who enjoy 2D pixel-art action platformers. They discover games on Itch.io, through streamers, Discord communities, and word-of-mouth. They value tight combat, retro aesthetics, dark fantasy atmosphere, and a "git gud" challenge.

## Product Purpose

CastleBounty is a 2D pixel-art action platformer built in Unity. The website is its primary marketing surface: to introduce the game, show gameplay and characters, build hype, and drive downloads / wishlists / community joins. Success looks like a visitor understanding the genre, vibe, and challenge within seconds, then clicking to play.

## Brand Personality

Dark fantasy, energetic, retro-cool, challenging, indie, mysterious. Tone: direct, dramatic, slightly ominous but exciting. Three words: **cursed**, **tight**, **legendary**.

## References

- https://szadiart.itch.io/ — rich pixel-art worlds, strong mood, atmospheric indie game pages
- https://milopanta.itch.io/king-in-the-mountain — cozy-yet-epic pixel fantasy presentation, Itch-native game identity

## Anti-references

Clean SaaS / dev-tool templates (Stripe, Vercel, Linear, Superhuman). Corporate enterprise UI. Too much whitespace. Generic startup landing pages. Pastel color palettes. Inter + gradients.

## Design Principles

1. **Atmosphere first** — the page should feel like a game screen, not a marketing template.
2. **Show, don't tell** — gameplay screenshots, characters, and pixel art carry the message.
3. **One strong accent** — purple/violet magic glow against a dark, haunted castle palette.
4. **Teen-friendly energy** — bold type, glows, subtle motion, clear CTAs.
5. **Itch.io-native** — works as a self-contained page, loads fast, feels at home next to indie games.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Keep text contrast high on dark surfaces. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Provide alt text for game screenshots and character sprites.
